Redesign this project for Jalashree Anglers Retreat, a family-run retreat in Kaliabor, Assam. It is NOT the Temps food shop. If this codebase contains that demo, replace its shop branding, product catalogue, cart, wishlist, checkout and shop policies. Do not carry its business details into Jalashree.

GOAL
A compact, Assamese-rooted resort homepage using the supplied Jalashree photographs. It should feel like the actual place, not an AI-generated luxury resort template. Calm, useful and short.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Warm ivory background, deep leaf green text and accents, a little muted terracotta red. Assam identity should come from the real bamboo entrance, jaapis, thatch, water, grounds and Assamese food.

Use one thin gamosa-inspired woven red detail as a restrained divider, not repeated borders around every card. No invented cultural symbols or scattered decorative icons.

One restrained serif for headings and a readable sans-serif for body text. If Assamese text is added, use proper glyph support and supplied translations.

Strong photo composition, simple alignment, small corner radii and controlled spacing. No dark gradients, glass cards, glow blobs, floating decorations, giant empty headings, animated counters, parallax or autoplay video.

FOUR SHORT BLOCKS
1. Compact header and hero: resort name, "Kaliabor, Assam", a bright real property photo and "Plan your visit" linking to the visit section. No full-screen hero or tall stacked navigation. On mobile, show the place in the first screen, with the next section beginning.

2. "By the water": one accommodation photo and a short introduction. The sign advertises lakeview cottages, but photos also show tents and thatched structures. Do not assume every structure is bookable accommodation.

3. "Spend the day": one compact photo-and-text composition covering fishing, Assamese meals and family picnics. These are advertised on the entrance sign; current availability needs confirmation. No generic three-card feature grid or full-height section for each activity.

4. "Plan your visit": Baruah Chuk, Kaliabor, Assam, with practical information once confirmed. Put extra photos in a small expandable gallery and detailed FAQs in accessible accordions.

LESS SCROLLING
Aim for roughly 3-4 mobile screens and 1-2 desktop screens at normal text size, not a rigid height cap. Use two columns on desktop where useful. No repeated CTAs, testimonial strip, long story section or giant footer. Do not shrink text, crop useful content or introduce horizontal scrolling to meet the target.

FACTS AND PREVIEW SAFETY
Preserve real photos and photographer credits. Do not invent prices, capacities, facilities, reviews, awards, hours, fishing rules or meal inclusions. The sign's phone numbers are not yet confirmed booking contacts. Do not wire up phone/WhatsApp or a map until the correct contact and map pin are supplied.

No fake availability, booking or payments. Keep one discreet "Design preview - booking not enabled" note. Put missing operational details in a developer checklist, not repeated warning paragraphs throughout the page.

IMPLEMENT AND CHECK
Inspect the existing code first and work within its stack. Use semantic HTML, accessible gallery/accordions, keyboard focus, readable contrast and comfortable tap targets. Optimise photos without distortion.

Inspect actual rendered screenshots at 390px mobile and 1280px desktop. Check image crops, wrapping, scroll length and every interaction. Deliver the updated preview and list the facts needed before launch. Do not publish a live booking site.
