About

Jalashree is a family-run anglers retreat in Baruah Chuk, Kaliabor. You come in through a bamboo arch. Two jaapis hang on the gate, and the sign sits between them.

Inside are open grounds, a stone path, thatch, and canvas tents near the water. The sign offers lakeview cottages, fishing, Assamese food, and picnics.
