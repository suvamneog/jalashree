# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS

## Users

People considering a visit to Jalashree Anglers Retreat: families, anglers, and day visitors looking at Kaliabor, Assam. Their job on this page is to recognise the place and see how a visit would be planned. They cannot book here.

## Product Purpose

A short homepage preview for a family-run anglers retreat. Success is a visitor understanding what the place is, what the entrance sign advertises, and where it is, without being offered a booking that does not exist.

## Positioning

Jalashree is a family-run retreat at Baruah Chuk, Kaliabor, Assam. The entrance sign advertises lakeview cottages, fishing, Assamese local cuisine, and picnic and family retreat. It is not a food shop, and it is not presented as a luxury resort chain.

## Operating Context

The family supplied photographs of the grounds, entrance, tents, thatched structures, water, and a meal, plus a logo. The live site does not exist yet. This page is a design preview for review before any public booking site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One homepage. No cart, catalogue, checkout, wishlist, or shop policies.
- No booking, payments, availability, phone link, WhatsApp link, or map until the family confirms the contact and the map pin.
- Do not invent prices, capacities, facilities, reviews, awards, hours, fishing rules, or meal inclusions.
- The entrance sign prints phone numbers. Those numbers are not confirmed booking contacts and must not be published as such.
- Address used on the page is the one on the entrance sign and in the brief: Baruah Chuk, Kaliabor, Assam. A logo sticker spells the village "Barua" and adds Nagaon. That difference is unresolved.
- Photographs are the supplied files. No photographer credit is embedded in them.
- One discreet note: design preview, booking not enabled.
- Missing operational facts belong in the developer checklist, not as repeated warnings in the page.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Jalashree Anglers Retreat. Place: Kaliabor, Assam. The supplied circular mark (fish, thatch, JAR) may be used. Binding visual constraints from the brief: warm ivory ground, deep leaf-green text and accents, a little muted terracotta, one thin gamosa-inspired woven red divider, one restrained serif for headings, a readable sans for body, real photographs of bamboo, jaapi, thatch, water, grounds, and Assamese food, small corner radii, compact page. No invented cultural symbols.

## Evidence on Hand

- Entrance sign text visible in the photographs: lakeview cottages, fishing experience, Assamese local cuisine, picnic and family retreat, Baruah Chuk, Kaliabor, Assam.
- Photographs in `WhatsApp Unknown 2026-10-13 at 2.37.11 PM/` and the logo files `logo.PNG`, `IMG_1631.JPG.jpeg`, `IMG_1632.JPG.jpeg`.
- Logo sticker also shows the line "Cast · Catch · Calm", the email jalashreeanglersretreat@gmail.com, and a phone number. None of these are confirmed for the public page.
- Absent, and not to be fabricated: rates, capacities, facilities, inclusions, hours, fishing rules, reviews, awards, confirmed booking contact, confirmed map pin, photographer credit, approved Assamese translation.

## Product Principles

- Show the real place, and only the facts that are already on the sign or in the photographs.
- Keep the page short enough to plan a visit, not to browse a resort brand.
- Say when something is not available yet, once, instead of decorating every section with a disclaimer.
- Do not turn unconfirmed structures into bookable rooms.
